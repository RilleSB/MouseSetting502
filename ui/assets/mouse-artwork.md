# Mouse illustration

`mouse-top.svg` and `mouse-side.svg` are independently drawn, editable vector
illustrations created for this project. They contain no photographs, traced
screenshot paths, manufacturer logos, embedded fonts, or external resources.
They depict a stylized eleven-button gaming mouse rather than an exact product
render. The geometry uses the same view boxes as the matching `mouse-light-*.svg`
overlays, which the application recolors for the lighting preview.

Artwork is provided under the repository's license. This documents the origin
of the artwork; it does not make a legal claim about third-party product designs
or trademarks elsewhere in the application.
